About Stackly

Wealth is built in layers. We help you stack them well.

Stackly is an investment and wealth planning firm based in Salem. We exist to make sound financial planning clear, fair and within reach for local families and professionals.

Our story

Good planning should not need a big-city address.

Too often, people outside the metros are offered products instead of plans. We started Stackly to change that: advice rooted in Salem, built on the same discipline that serious investors rely on, and explained in a way anyone can follow.

The name comes from a simple idea. Wealth rarely arrives in one leap. It is stacked, one SIP, one goal and one good decision at a time.
