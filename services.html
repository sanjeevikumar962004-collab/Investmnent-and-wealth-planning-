Our approach

One idea: match the mix to the goal.

These example mixes show how risk comfort and time horizon change the balance between growth and stability. They are illustrations, not recommendations. Your own allocation depends on your goals, timeline and comfort with ups and downs.

Steady

For shorter goals and a low tolerance for swings.

  • Equity 25%
  • Debt 60%
  • Gold 10%
  • Cash 5%

Balanced

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Do I need a large amount to begin?
No. Many mutual funds allow SIPs to start with a small monthly amount. What matters more is starting early and staying consistent. We will help you pick an amount that fits your budget.
Are returns guaranteed?
No. Market-linked investments carry risk and their value can go up or down. We plan around realistic assumptions and show you the range of outcomes rather than a single promise.
How are you paid?
We share how every service is charged, in writing, before you commit. If there is any cost or conflict of interest connected with a recommendation, we tell you up front.
Can you work with the investments I already have?
Yes. We start with a neutral review of your existing funds, policies and savings, and keep what works. Changes are suggested only where they help your goals.
Do I have to meet in person?
We love meeting in Salem, but video and phone calls work well too. Documents can be shared and signed digitally where allowed.
How often will we review my plan?
At least once a year, and whenever something big changes, like a new job, a child, a home purchase or a business milestone.
