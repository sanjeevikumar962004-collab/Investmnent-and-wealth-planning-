Privacy Policy & Advisory Standards

How Stackly collects, protects, and handles your financial information with fiduciary rigor and absolute confidentiality.

Fiduciary Commitment: At Stackly, we treat your financial life with the highest duty of care. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third party for marketing or cross-selling purposes.

1. Information We Collect

When you engage with Stackly for goal planning, mutual fund investments, or wealth advisory, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill our advisory and statutory obligations:

  • Identification & KYC Data: Full name, date of birth, contact details (email and mobile number), government identity numbers (such as PAN or Aadhaar where required by regulations) for investment registration and KYC compliance.
  • Financial & Goal Data: Income brackets, investment timelines, life goals (retirement, child education, home purchase), and risk tolerance metrics.
  • Portal Interaction Data: Login activity, session timestamps, and device metrics to maintain security and prevent unauthorized access to your client portal.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for authorized financial planning and client management activities:

  • Constructing customized, goal-based investment plans and SIP roadmaps.
  • Processing mutual fund, debt, or insurance advisory requests in accordance with Indian securities frameworks.
  • Providing regular portfolio statements, rebalancing reminders, and performance reviews.
  • Communicating important tax and regulatory updates that directly affect your holdings.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement bank-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of your sensitive wealth information:

  • All digital transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Client portal credentials and sensitive documents are protected by multi-layered authorization protocols.
  • Internal access to client files is restricted strictly to your designated advisory team on a need-to-know basis.

4. Terms of Advisory & Statutory Compliance

By consulting Stackly or utilizing our advisory portal, clients acknowledge and agree to our core operating standards:

  • Risk Disclosure: Investments in mutual funds and capital market instruments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing. Historical performance does not guarantee future results.
  • Fee Transparency: All advisory fees, planning costs, or distributor arrangements are disclosed in writing prior to plan execution. There are no hidden charges.
  • Regulatory Jurisdiction: Stackly operates in compliance with SEBI and AMFI guidelines, subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Salem, Tamil Nadu, India.

We retain financial and transactional records in accordance with Indian tax and financial regulatory requirements. If you have questions regarding your data, privacy preferences, or wish to update your records, please contact our privacy desk:
